# Tâi-lô Practice

Terms for the learner practice and mistake-history domain.

## Learner history

**Mistake record**:
A record of a practice question the learner answered incorrectly, including the learner's answer and the expected answer.
_Avoid_: Error, failed attempt

**Mistake history**:
The collection of a learner's mistake records across practice units.
_Avoid_: Review data

**Fixed mistake**:
A mistake record for a question the learner later answers correctly. A subsequent incorrect answer reopens the mistake.
_Avoid_: Deleted mistake, completed record

**Sandhi editorial review**:
The human review and curation of tone-sandhi question data, separate from a learner's mistake history.
_Avoid_: Learner review, mistake review
